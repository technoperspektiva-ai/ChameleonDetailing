ChameleonDetailing v1.1.47 UPDATE — Order Payments + Google Sheets

1. Order/request cards now include a 💳 Payments button, including new-order notifications in personal staff chats and the connected order chat.
2. Payments flow: Payments → ✅ Paid → choose 💵 Cash or 💳 Card.
3. Marking a payment creates a PAID row in D1 payments with the order amount, selected method, staff marker and paid timestamp.
4. service_requests.payment_status is updated to PAID and the payment method is shown in the order card.
5. Duplicate PAID rows are prevented by checking for an existing paid payment before insert.
6. Payment actions work in the shared order chat without exposing the management panel.
7. Google Sheets Payments/Revenue reports pick up the new D1 payment rows on the next manual or automatic sync.
8. Existing Google Sheets via Apps Script integration and Excel reports remain unchanged.
