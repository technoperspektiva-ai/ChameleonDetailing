ChameleonDetailing v1.2.2 — Garage & Saved Car UX

• Added a dedicated My Garage tab directly after Home in the bottom navigation.
• Saved cars now store and expose a canonical body/vehicle type.
• Selecting a saved car in the calculator automatically applies its vehicle type.
• When the car already defines the vehicle type, the calculator skips the redundant vehicle-type step.
• Car creation/editing now uses a clear body-type selector instead of free-form text.
• Added a brand selector with compact brand badges shown in the garage and calculator car picker.
• Garage cards show brand/model/modification, body type, plate, ceramic status and visit count.
• Each car now has a dedicated Completed Work view.
• Completed Work shows all main services, selected options, staff-added services, completion date and final amount.
• Car history endpoint now returns completed work only and includes staff-added extras.
• Existing car package logic remains available and can still be applied from the calculator.
• GitHub production build verified successfully.
