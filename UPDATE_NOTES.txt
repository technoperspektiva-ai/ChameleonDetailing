ChameleonDetailing v1.2.4 — Approved Icons & Garage Interaction

• Integrated the user-provided approved ChameleonDetailing icon pack for base services and supported add-ons.
• Approved icons are packaged as one optimized transparent sprite to keep their visual style consistent and avoid missing binary assets.
• Admin-provided custom service images still take priority; approved icons are used for the standard catalog.
• Saved car cards are now collapsed by default.
• Tapping a car opens a compact action panel instead of showing all controls permanently.
• Edit and delete are compact icon actions inside the expanded car panel.
• Estimate remains available as a compact action.
• A separate full-width Completed Orders button sits at the bottom of the expanded car panel.
• Bottom navigation labels have been restored under the icons.
• Existing garage history, service recency, reminders, automatic vehicle type and model catalog remain intact.
