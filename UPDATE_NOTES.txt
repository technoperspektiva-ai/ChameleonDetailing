ChameleonDetailing v1.2.3 — Garage Polish & Service Recency

• Bottom navigation is now icon-first and visually lighter; labels stay accessible but are hidden from the compact UI.
• Garage action buttons use restrained Lucide icons and shorter labels instead of emoji-heavy controls.
• Completed-work view now shows the last completion date for each service on a saved car.
• Cars not serviced for roughly 90+ days receive a subtle, friendly maintenance reminder.
• Added a structured car brand/model catalog for the vehicle form, plus an Other Model fallback.
• The catalog includes mainstream, premium and performance brands used across Europe and international markets.
• Added future-ready model icon lookup: /car-models/<brand-model>.webp automatically displays when an original model asset exists, otherwise the brand badge is used.
• Existing automatic body-type selection in the calculator remains active.
• Production build verified successfully before release.
