ChameleonDetailing v1.1.49 UPDATE — Home Socials + Professional Specialists

1. Added a premium Social Media section to the Mini App home page.
2. Social networks use lime/neon line icons and the same rounded dark surfaces as the rest of Chameleon Detailing.
3. Owner/Admin can configure Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp and Website URLs.
4. Every social network can be enabled/disabled individually, and the entire Social Media home block can also be hidden.
5. Added a Professional Specialists section to the Mini App home page.
6. Each specialist card can contain: name, role/title, portfolio URL, optional external/social URL, a custom link label, and an optional photo URL.
7. Owner/Admin can add, edit, enable/disable or delete specialists, plus show/hide the whole specialists section.
8. Mini App refreshes social/specialist visibility while open, so staff changes appear without forcing the client to restart Telegram.
9. Added /api/specialists and extended /api/socials to respect the home visibility settings.
10. Existing order Payments, localized Excel reports and Google Sheets integrations remain unchanged.

Admin paths:
Settings -> Social Media
Settings -> Specialists

Redeploy the Cloudflare Worker after updating the repository.
