ChameleonDetailing v1.2.0 — Multi-service, Cars, Dynamic Add-ons & Referral Rewards

• Calculator now supports multiple main services in one request.
• Service-specific logic controls whether vehicle, condition and extras are required.
• Add-ons are linked to specific main services and filtered dynamically.
• Added expanded detailing add-on catalog with a unified lime-green icon system.
• Added My Cars: name, brand, model, modification, body type, plate, ceramic flag and owner phone.
• A saved car can keep a default work package and be reused in the calculator.
• Added per-car service history.
• Requests store all selected services, options and selected car snapshot.
• Owner/Admin service panel now controls condition/vehicle/options and linked add-ons.
• Referral rewards can be configured for referrer + invited friend and granted on COMPLETED or PAID.
• Referral rewards are automatically applied to eligible quotes and consumed on submitted order.
• Telegram order cards show localized service names, options and saved car details.
• Google Sheets / Excel reports now include cars, car packages, multi-service orders and referral rewards.
• Reviews and Suggestions continue to sync into Google Sheets.
• Project reset clears new client operational data while preserving business configuration.
• GitHub CI and Cloudflare production build verified successfully before release.
